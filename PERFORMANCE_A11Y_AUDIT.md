## What this does and does not cover

- axe-core finds roughly a third to a half of WCAG issues. It cannot judge whether alt text is *meaningful*, whether focus order makes sense to a screen-reader user, or whether an error message is understandable. Manual keyboard and screen-reader passes are on the UAT checklist.
- Flows are audited in demo/mock fallback, because that is what runs without owner credentials. The live variants render additional panels; re-run this against staging once Supabase is configured.
- The QR-scan flow audits the counter dialog in its post-scan state; camera capture is out of scope for this MVP by design.
